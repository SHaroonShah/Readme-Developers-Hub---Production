To improve clarity and reduce ambiguity across regions using different date conventions, the date format on Commercial and Proforma Invoices generated via the **PrintDocuments API** has been updated from **DD/MM/YYYY to DD Mmm YYYY** (for example, 04 Oct 2026).

This change applies to:

- Invoice Date
- Declaration of Origin

The updated format helps ensure invoice dates are interpreted consistently regardless of local date formatting standards.
