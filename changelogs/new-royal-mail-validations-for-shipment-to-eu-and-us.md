The following fields have been made mandatory for any Royal Mail shipments destined to EU and US:

<Table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>
        Destination
      </th>

      <th>
        Mandatory Fields
      </th>
    </tr>
  </thead>

  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>
        European Union (EU)
      </td>

      <td>
        - **SKUCode**
        - **ManufactureProductId**
      </td>
    </tr>

    <tr>
      <td>
        United States (US)
      </td>

      <td>
        Shipper **ContactEmail** or **ContactPhone**
      </td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</Table>
