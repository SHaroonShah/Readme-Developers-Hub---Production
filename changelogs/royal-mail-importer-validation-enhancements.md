The importer validation rules for Royal Mail international shipments has been updated to provide greater flexibility when supplying importer details:&#x20;

- Importer <Glossary>EORI</Glossary> and <Glossary>VAT</Glossary> numbers are no longer mandatory for <Glossary>DTP</Glossary> and <Glossary>IOSS</Glossary>/DTP shipments when importer information is provided.
- Importer VAT numbers are no longer required for <Glossary>DDP</Glossary> B2B shipments when importer details are supplied.
- Customers can now provide importer details that differ from the recipient's details.
- Importer information provided in the shipment request will be passed through and used in downstream customs processes.
