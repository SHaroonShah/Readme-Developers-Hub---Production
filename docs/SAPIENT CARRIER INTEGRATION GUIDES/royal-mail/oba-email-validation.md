To use Intersoft's Royal Mail label service, you must have an account set up with the Royal Mail Group. We need to request approval from Royal Mail to set up your account with us. Once your Royal Mail Account is set up, you can submit an integration request.

Provide the information below so that your account with us can be set up accordingly.

## Check OBA account activation

An active OBA account must be provided for Intersoft to set up your account.

> 🚧 *Important*
>
> *If you do not log in to your OBA account for over 90 days, Royal Mail will deactivate your OBA account.*

To check whether your OBA account is active, follow the steps below.

<Accordion title="Step 1: Access the Royal Mail OBA website" icon="fa-globe">

Go to the Royal Mail [Online Business Account website](https://www.royalmail.com/discounts-payment/credit-account/online-business-account).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Step 2: Log in with your credentials" icon="fa-sign-in-alt">

On the login page, enter your **email address** and **password**, then select **Log in**.

<Image align="center" alt="Logging into RM OBA" border={true} caption="Logging into RM OBA" src="https://files.readme.io/ba7290711628e12af9e9a6131ca7fc9cbc8c63b417aff2dbd720fe1c6bce51b9-OBA_login_page.png" width="600px" />

> 📘 *Note*
>
> *If you do not know which email address is associated with your OBA account, please contact the OBA Helpdesk—call at 08457 950 950, or send an email at [oba.helpdesk@royalmail.com](mailto:oba.helpdesk@royalmail.com).*

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Step 3: Access your Online Business Account" icon="fa-check-circle">

Once logged in, select **Access Online Business Account**.

<Image align="center" alt="Accessing OBA" border={true} caption="Accessing OBA" src="https://files.readme.io/463e646-image.png" />

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="✅ Account is active">

If your OBA account is active, you are directed to your online business account dashboard. Enter this OBA email address into our customer onboarding form.

After accessing your OBA account, you can view your billing and reports for tracked services.

<Image align="center" alt="Displaying OBA" border={true} caption="Displaying OBA dashboard" src="https://files.readme.io/8bf81a9-image.png" />

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="❌ Account is not active">

If your account is not active, the page will either reload (returning you to the same page) or redirect you to a page headed **SAP NetWeaver**.

> 🚧 *Important*
>
> *If your OBA account is not active, contact Royal Mail to reactivate it via the following link: [https://business.help.royalmail.com/app/contact](https://business.help.royalmail.com/app/contact).*

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

</Accordion>
