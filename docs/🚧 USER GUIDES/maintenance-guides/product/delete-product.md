In SAPIENT, you can delete a product to cleanup the database, preventing confusion with outdated products. This process potentially improves system performance, as fewer entries can lead to faster processing and operations.

To delete the product in SAPIENT, follow the steps as explained in the following procedure.

1. In the left navigation panel, select **Products**. 

<Image alt="Accessing shipping locations" align="center" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/ec355529da5603569fe7c27204b1b58b5f7368185e1daa06e9836a6a0c4e855b-Acccessing_products_option.png">
  Accessing products
</Image>

2. On the **Products** page that appears, from the list, select the product that you want to delete, and then in the **Edit Product** form that opens, click ![alt text](https://files.readme.io/63292b906edfcaf8554e4c4b9385b1ba483ec4ada1541955dde6ff5a78cc55fe-Delete_product_button.png).

<Image alt="Selecting option to add shipping location" align="center" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/c84f99786468144347b5039b2c1bde7322000a27e08f78cb070a4fbd17dbd9f0-Delete_product_option.png">
  Deleting product
</Image>

3. In the confirmation dialog that appears, select **Yes** to confirm your action. 

<Image alt="Confirming product deletion" align="center" width="350px" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/2c4dc02c1409b059556e92db227a058f328c7cfb5de80c36e95021287647f311-Confirming_product_deletion.png">
  Confirming product deletion
</Image>

Once confirmed, the product is deleted successfully and no longer displays in the **Product** list.

> 📘 *Note*
>
> *Products can be removed via API. To learn more on how to delete a product via API, refer to the[API References](https://docs.intersoftsapient.net/reference/post_v4-products) section.*
