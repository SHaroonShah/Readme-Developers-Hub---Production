A product is any physical item or good that is being transported from one location to another. This can include a wide range of items, from consumer goods and industrial materials to perishable items and fragile products.

The shipping of these products often requires specific handling, packaging, and transportation methods based on their unique characteristics.

<Accordion title="Product Handling Considerations" icon="info-circle">

When managing products in your shipping system, consider these important factors:

### Physical Characteristics
- **Size and weight** - Affects packaging and transportation methods
- **Fragility** - Determines protective measures needed
- **Shape** - Impacts stacking and storage efficiency

### Special Requirements
- **Temperature sensitivity** - May require refrigerated transport
- **Hazardous materials** - Need special handling and documentation
- **Perishable items** - Require expedited shipping timelines

### Documentation
- **Product codes** - For inventory tracking and identification
- **Handling instructions** - Clear guidelines for warehouse staff
- **Shipping restrictions** - Any limitations on transport methods
