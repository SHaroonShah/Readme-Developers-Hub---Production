## What is a User?

A _user_ refers to an individual or entity that interacts with shipping software to manage shipping operations, track shipments, or perform related logistics tasks.

<Accordion title="User Types & Roles" icon="users">
Users can include a variety of roles such as:

- **Shippers** - Manage outbound shipments and logistics
- **Warehouse Staff** - Handle inventory and fulfillment operations
- **Logistics Managers** - Oversee shipping operations and strategy
- **Customer Service Representatives** - Assist with shipment tracking and issues
- **Third-party Service Providers** - External partners and vendors

Each user typically has specific permissions and access levels based on their role within the organization.
</Accordion>
