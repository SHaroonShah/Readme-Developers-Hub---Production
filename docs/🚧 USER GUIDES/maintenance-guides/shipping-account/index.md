A **shipping account** is a specific account set up with a shipping carrier or logistics provider that enables businesses to manage shipping activities. This centralized system allows you to handle all aspects of your shipping operations efficiently.

## Manage Your Shipping Accounts

<Cards columns="3">
  <Card title="Add Account" href="https://docs.intersoftsapient.net/docs/add-a-shipping-account" icon="plus">
    Set up a new shipping account with your preferred carrier or logistics provider to start processing shipments.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Edit Account" href="https://docs.intersoftsapient.net/docs/edit-shipping-account" icon="edit">
    Modify existing shipping account details, update credentials, or change configuration settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Delete Account" href="https://docs.intersoftsapient.net/docs/delete-shipping-account" icon="trash">
    Remove shipping accounts that are no longer needed or have been deactivated.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## Key Benefits

<Accordion title="Streamlined Operations" icon="cogs">
Centralize all your shipping activities in one place, making it easier to track and manage multiple carrier relationships.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Cost Management" icon="dollar-sign">
Better negotiate rates and track shipping costs across different carriers and service levels.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Integration Capabilities" icon="link">
Seamlessly integrate with your existing systems for automated shipping label generation and tracking updates.
</Accordion>
