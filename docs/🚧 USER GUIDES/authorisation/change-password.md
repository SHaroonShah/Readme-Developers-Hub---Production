To change your password securely, follow the step-by-step procedure below.

<Accordion title="🔒 Important Security Guidelines" icon="shield-alt">

Before you set or change your password, ensure the following:

1. **Use a password manager** to secure your password in an encrypted format
2. **Include a mix of characters** - combine uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters
3. **Avoid common words or phrases** that could be easily guessed
4. **Monitor for breaches** regularly

For more detailed information on creating secure passwords, refer to the [guidelines for choosing a strong password](https://docs.intersoftsapient.net/docs/guidelines-for-choosing-strong-password#/) section.

</Accordion>

## How to Change Your Password

<Cards columns={2}>
  <Card title="Step 1: Access Change Password" icon="user-cog">
    In the application header, select the user icon. In the dropdown menu that opens, select **Change Password**.

  <Card title="Step 2: Fill Out the Form" icon="edit">

## What Happens Next

Once you complete the form and submit your changes, you'll be automatically redirected to the Sign in page. This confirms that your password has been successfully changed. You can now log in using your new password.

<Accordion title="💡 Pro Tips" icon="lightbulb">

- **Keep your password private** - Never share it with anyone
- **Update regularly** - Consider changing your password periodically
- **Use unique passwords** - Don't reuse passwords across different platforms
- **Enable two-factor authentication** if available for additional security

</Accordion>
