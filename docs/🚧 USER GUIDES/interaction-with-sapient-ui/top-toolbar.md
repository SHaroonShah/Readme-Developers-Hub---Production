Depending on the instance, the SAPIENT's top toolbar accommodates different elements, the main of which are: 

- **Breadcrumbs**: (also known as breadcrumb trail) is your navigation assistance that links  to a previous page or pages you have navigated and indicates your current location within the application. You can also click ![alt text](https://files.readme.io/5cbcc9024801646cae18edc6965107b0be004ce243a4833b552e89e16521a197-Home_button.png) that takes you directly to the application's home page.
- **Workspace toolbar**: the workspace toolbar is in the top toolbar's top-right hand corner. The options may vary depending on the object and action type, for example, **Add Shipping Account**, **Delete Product**, **Back to Shipping Locations**, and so on.
