In SAPIENT, the primary purpose of the **Shipment Processing** feature is to allow users to track and control the status of shipments as they move through different stages of the shipping process, ensuring efficiency and accuracy in logistics management.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/2148c6141328734d3e4f852fdfc27e8ef114486ae0bacf6b754daaf8111a53bd-Shipment_processing.png" align="center" width="350px" border={true} />


With the shipment processing feature, you can monitor and track the details of your shipments and do the following:

<Cards>
  <Card title="Cancel a Shipment" href="https://docs.intersoftsapient.net/docs/view-cancelled-shipments" icon="fa-solid fa-nfc-slash" target="_blank">
    Stop a shipment that is no longer needed or has issues that prevent delivery.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Recall a Shipment" href="https://docs.intersoftsapient.net/docs/recall-shipment" icon="fa-solid fa-undo" target="_blank">
    Retrieve a shipment that has already been processed but needs to be brought back.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Hold a Shipment" href="https://docs.intersoftsapient.net/docs/held-shipments" icon="fa-solid fa-circle-pause" target="_blank">
    Temporarily pause a shipment for review or to address any issues before proceeding.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Release a Shipment" href="https://docs.intersoftsapient.net/docs/release-shipment" icon="fa-solid fa-circle-play" target="_blank">
    Resume processing of a held shipment once issues are resolved or review is complete.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Manifest a Shipment" href="https://docs.intersoftsapient.net/docs/manifesting-shipments" icon="fa-solid fa-truck-ramp-box" target="_blank">
    Generate and process the shipping manifest for final shipment preparation.
  </Card>

  <Card title="View Manifest History" href="https://docs.intersoftsapient.net/v4.04/docs/manifest-history#/versions" icon="fa-solid fa-file" target="_blank">
    View a comprehensive record of all shipping manifests generated over time.
  </Card>
</Cards>
